What your band means

Band 9 — Expert

Fully operational command of English: accurate, fluent and complete.

Band 8 — Very good

Fully operational with only occasional unsystematic inaccuracies.

Band 7 — Good

Operational command; handles complex language well despite some errors.

Band 6 — Competent

Generally effective command despite some inaccuracies and misunderstandings.

Band 5 — Modest

Partial command; copes with overall meaning in most situations.

Band 4 — Limited

Basic competence limited to familiar situations; frequent problems.
